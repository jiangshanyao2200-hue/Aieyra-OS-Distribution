Supply either path (one image) or paths (2–12), never both. Paths are literal, relative to workdir or absolute. Multiple images become one numbered contact sheet; layout=grid(default)/row/column. The result maps numbered tiles to sources and dimensions. Prefer contact sheets for comparison; inspect a source separately for illegible small details. Stitching does not guarantee lower provider billing.
Each source: 8 MiB, 8192 per side, 32 million pixels. Multiple sources: 48 MiB and 64 million pixels total. Composite maximum edge 2048, alpha on neutral background.
