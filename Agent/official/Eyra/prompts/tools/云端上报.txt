cloud_report arguments: {action, ...fields}. report_bug {title, summary, content, request_id, confirmed:true} publishes a public bug topic to the signed-in account’s Aieyra HOME community. This is a public account mutation; confirmed records authorization and does not grant additional permissions. receipt {request_id} reads the original submission status; identical retries retain that request_id. update_status / update_check read official update state; other update_<action> map to the updates service (review, read_file, resolve_file, prepare, verify, decide), with signature, merge and permission checks enforced by that service.
